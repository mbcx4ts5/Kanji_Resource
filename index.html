Section 1: Essential Kanji

In the first section, we will consider the most essential kanji and the elements that comprise them. We will aim to cover every kanji that a Japanese pupil would encounter in the first three grades of elementary school alongside a few kanji from later years that are useful to learn early. These kanji and their elements form the backbone of the most common and useful words in Japanese.
